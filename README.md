# Sagarika In Snapshots

Build an extraordinary, deeply personal, and emotional interactive birthday website for my sister, [Sister's Name], who is turning [Age] tomorrow. Make the aesthetic warm, dreamy, editorial, and nostalgic—like a luxury digital scrapbook meets a modern Apple-style interactive story.

### Visual Design & Theme:

- **Color Palette:** Warm cream/ivory background (#FAF6F0), soft blush and rose gold accents (#E8B4B8, #C88A7E), deep charcoal serif typography (#2C2523) for elegance, paired with clean sans-serif body text.

- **Typography:** Use an editorial serif font (like Playfair Display or Cormorant Garamond) for headings and quotes, and a clean sans-serif (like Plus Jakarta Sans) for story text.

- **Animations:** Smooth scroll-triggered fade-ins, subtle floating bokeh/confetti particles on load, gentle parallax effects on photos, and tactile hover states.

### Page Structure & Interactive Features:

1. **Hero Section (Full-Screen Opening):**

   - A cinematic opening with soft floating sparkles/confetti.

   - Headline: "Happy Birthday, [Sister's Name] ✨"

   - Subheadline: "A little corner of the internet dedicated to my favorite person and all our memories."

   - Include a floating, aesthetic **Mini Music Player** pill in the corner with a custom playlist feel (play/pause toggle with soft ambient visual equalizer bars).

2. **Interactive "Memory Polaroid" Gallery (Scrapbook Section):**

   - Create a masonry/scattered Polaroid-style photo grid where photos look slightly tilted like real printed memories with subtle tape/shadow details.

   - Include 8–10 placeholder photo cards that I can easily replace with our real photos.

   - **Interactive Feature:** Clicking any Polaroid opens a sleek modal/lightbox showing the full photo, the year/date, and a handwritten-style caption/backstory on the side.

   - Include filter tabs at the top: "All Memories", "Childhood", "Best Trips", "Random Chaos".

3. **"Reasons Why You're the Best Sister" (Interactive Card Deck / Flip Cards):**

   - A 3x2 grid of interactive flip cards.

   - Front of each card shows a number and a teaser (e.g., "01. My Forever Partner-in-Crime", "02. Best Advice Giver", etc.).

   - Hovering or clicking flips the card smoothly in 3D to reveal a heartfelt note or funny inside joke.

4. **Our Timeline (Vertical Interactive Story):**

   - A clean vertical timeline with a glowing progress line as you scroll, highlighting key milestones from childhood to today, each with a small photo thumbnail, year badge, and memory blurb.

5. **Interactive "Make a Wish" Birthday Cake:**

   - A beautifully illustrated/CSS-crafted minimalist birthday cake with glowing flickering candles.

   - Include a glowing button: **"Blow Out the Candles 🎂"**.

   - When clicked, the candle flames extinguish with a puff of smoke, celebratory confetti bursts across the screen, and a secret hidden birthday message unlocks below.

6. **A Heartfelt Letter Section:**

   - Styled like textured stationery paper with a wax-seal emblem at the bottom.

   - Write a warm, emotional placeholder letter from her brother that I can customize, signed off with "Always in your corner, [Your Name]".

### Technical & UX Polish:

- Ensure 100% mobile responsiveness (she will likely open this on her phone first).

- Use Lucide icons, Framer Motion for buttery animations, and clean modular components so I can easily upload and swap my real images into the gallery and timeline.
